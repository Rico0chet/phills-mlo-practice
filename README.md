# MLO Exam Prep

I want to make a practice exam for MLO licensing. this will be broken down in chapters 1-10. Each chapter will have a separate page. Lets start with chapter 1 page 1. The questions in chapter 1 page 1 are as followed

1. Which of the following best describes a conventional loan?

A. A loan insured by the FHA
B. A loan guaranteed by the VA
C. A loan offered by private lenders and not insured or guaranteed by the government
D. A USDA-backed rural housing loan

2. Conventional conforming loans must meet the guidelines established by:

A. HUD only

B. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac

C. FHA and VA

D. CFPB

3. What is the primary difference between a conforming and a non-conforming loan?

A. Down payment requirements

B. Interest rate type

C. Whether the loan meets GSE guidelines and loan limits

D. Property location

4. As of 2024, the conforming loan limit for a single-family home in most areas was approximately:

A. $510,400

B. $650,000

C. $726,200

D. $1,000,000

5. Conventional loans generally require a minimum down payment of:

A. 0%

B. 1%

C. 3%–5%

D. 20%

6. Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) is generally required when the borrower puts down:

A. Less than 5%

B. Less than 10%

C. Less than 15%

D. Less than 20%

7. PMI primarily protects:

A. The borrower

B. The seller

C. The lender

D. The title company

8. A borrower may generally request PMI removal when their loan-to-value (LTV) reaches:

A. 95%

B. 90%

C. 85%

D. 80%

9. Which mortgage provides the greatest payment stability?

A. Adjustable Rate Mortgage (ARM)

B. Interest-Only Loan

C. Fixed-Rate Mortgage

D. Jumbo Loan

10. A 7/1 ARM typically means:

A. The loan lasts seven years.

B. The interest rate is fixed for seven years before adjusting.

C. Payments are made every seven months.

D. The loan adjusts every seven years immediately.

11. Which loan term usually results in higher monthly payments but lower total interest paid?

A. 30-year mortgage

B. 40-year mortgage

C. 15-year mortgage

D. Interest-only mortgage

12. Conventional loan limits are established annually by:

A. HUD

B. CFPB

C. Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA)

D. FHA

13. What minimum credit score is typically required for a conforming conventional loan?

A. 580

B. 600

C. 620

D. 700

14. A debt-to-income (DTI) ratio of ______ is generally considered acceptable for a conventional loan.

A. 28%

B. 36%

C. 43%

D. 55%

15. Self-employed borrowers are often required to provide:

A. Military discharge papers

B. Profit and loss statements and tax returns

C. Vehicle registration

D. College transcripts

16. Which document verifies that gifted down-payment funds do not have to be repaid?

A. Closing Disclosure

B. Gift Letter

C. Promissory Note

D. HUD-1 Settlement Statement

17. Which step of the mortgage process involves the lender evaluating risk and deciding whether to approve the loan?

A. Closing

B. Appraisal

C. Underwriting

D. Pre-Approval

18. What is the primary purpose of a property appraisal?

A. Determine the borrower's credit score

B. Verify employment

C. Estimate the property's market value

D. Calculate closing costs

19. Which federal law requires lenders to clearly disclose loan costs and terms?

A. RESPA

B. ECOA

C. TILA (Truth in Lending Act)

D. HMDA

20. Which federal law prohibits discrimination in lending based on race, religion, sex, marital status, national origin, and other protected characteristics?

A. TILA

B. ECOA

C. SAFE Act

D. FDCPA
